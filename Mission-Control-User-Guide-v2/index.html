Welcome to Mission Control

Mission Control v2.4.2 is your command center for orchestrating AI agents, managing tasks, and accelerating everything you build — from a single chat with your AI to coordinating entire agent swarms. This is User Guide v2, refreshed for the current portal.

This guide walks you through every panel, control, and workflow in the portal. Whether you're logging in for the first time or exploring the deeper controls, you'll find a dedicated section for the top bar and for each tab in the left sidebar — grouped into Main, Work, Agents, Workspace, Knowledge, and Mods.

Getting Started

First Login

Your first login sets up your AI partnership. Here's exactly what to expect and how to get oriented quickly.

1
Go to app.purebrain.ai
Open the portal in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox recommended). The portal supports both light and dark themes — switch anytime from the top bar — and is optimized for desktop use.
2
Enter your credentials
Use the email and password from your PureBrain invitation. If you signed up via the website, use those same credentials. Forgot your password? Use the reset link on the login screen.
3
You land on Chat by default
The portal opens on the Chat tab — your primary AI conversation surface. The left sidebar shows every tab, grouped into Main / Work / Agents / Workspace / Knowledge / Mods. The top bar shows the portal version (v2.4.2) plus Dock Chat, the context meter, Online status, and Restart — see The Top Bar & Global Controls.
4
Check the Hub for system status
Click Hub in the left sidebar. You'll see your civilization's name, uptime, and resource gauges (Context, Memory, CPU, Disk). Green = healthy. If anything shows red, check Connected APIs.
5
Set up your profile
Go to Settings → Profile to review your name, email, and civilization details. Head to Settings → Preferences to configure notification and display preferences.
Bookmark the Hub

The Hub tab is your system health dashboard. Bookmark it or keep it open in a second window during your first sessions — it shows real-time agent activity and resource usage.

First 5 Steps

The fastest path from first login to your first AI win. Do these five things in your first session and you'll be operating at full speed.

1
Send your first Chat message
Click Chat in the sidebar. Type a task you'd normally do yourself — "summarize my last week's emails", "draft a LinkedIn post about AI agents", or "help me plan my product roadmap." Hit Enter. Your AI civilization responds immediately.
2
Try Let's Talk (voice)
Click Let's Talk in the sidebar. Click the microphone button and say your request out loud. The AI responds in your chosen voice. This is the fastest way to interact — no typing required.
3
Read your Constitution
Click Constitution → Global AI Rules. Read what rules are already in place. These govern how your AI operates. Understand them before you modify them — they're your AI's behavioral DNA.

Browse the Skills Shop
Click Skills in the sidebar. Browse available skills by category — Research, Content, Code, Operations. Activating a skill gives your agents new reusable capabilities. Start with 1-2 that match your immediate goals.

Click Agents → My Active Agents. You'll see your agent fleet — who's active, who's idle, and what each one specializes in. Click AI Organogram to see the full hierarchy: conductor → team leads → specialists.
The 10-minute orientation

Spend exactly 10 minutes: 3 min in Chat (send a real task), 2 min in Let's Talk (try voice), 2 min in Constitution (read Global Rules), 2 min in Skills (browse categories), 1 min in Agents (see your fleet). You'll know the portal.

How to Talk to Your AI

Your AI responds to natural language — not commands, not code. But knowing a few patterns unlocks dramatically better results.

The Four Communication Modes

Voice (Let's Talk)
Speak naturally. Ideal for quick requests, brainstorming, status checks. No setup required.
Text (Chat)
Type requests of any length. Best for detailed specs, multi-step tasks, and reviewing outputs.
Task Assignment
Create structured tasks in the Tasks tab. Agents pick them up, execute, and report back.
Team Launch (Teams)
Spin up a multi-agent swarm for complex work. Watch agents collaborate in live panes.

Prompt Patterns That Work

Your AI is most effective when your request specifies what, format, and context. Here are patterns to try:

Research Request
Research the top 3 competitors to [product], find their pricing pages, and give me a comparison table with pros/cons for each.
Content Creation
Write a LinkedIn post about [topic]. Tone: professional but warm. Length: ~200 words. End with a question to drive comments.
Task Decomposition
I need to launch a new product feature by end of month. Break this into a sprint plan — tasks, priorities, dependencies, and estimated effort per item.
Deep Analysis
Analyze the attached document and identify: (1) key decisions made, (2) open questions, (3) risks, and (4) recommended next steps. Be concise — bullet points preferred.

The Most Important Habit

Treat your AI like a highly capable colleague, not a search engine. Don't ask questions — assign work. Instead of "What are good marketing strategies?" say "Create a 30-day marketing plan for [my product] targeting [audience] with a $5K budget."

The "back and forth" principle

The soul of PureBrain is in the dialogue. Send a request, review the output, then refine: "Good — now make the tone more direct" or "Add a section on risks." Iteration produces the best results. Your AI remembers context within a session.

Multi-AI Patterns

When a task is too large for one agent, use the Teams tab to launch a swarm. Common patterns:

PatternWhen to UseHow
Parallel ResearchNeed multiple angles researched simultaneouslyTeams → launch swarm, each agent covers one angle
Draft + ReviewQuality-critical contentAgent 1 drafts, Agent 2 critiques, Agent 3 finalizes
Plan + ExecuteComplex projectsChat to plan, Teams to execute parallel workstreams
Overnight RunsLong-running research or buildsSet up Tasks + BOOPs to run while you sleep

Let's Talk

The voice-first entry point to your AI civilization. Let's Talk is a full Human-Machine Interface (HMI) for natural, spoken conversation with your AI — no keyboard required.

Voice Interface

The Let's Talk screen centers on a live audio visualizer — a canvas animation that responds to your microphone in real time. Around it:

ControlWhat it does
Microphone button (🎤)Toggle listening on/off. Click to start, click again to stop and send.
Standby / Listen / SendManual state controls. Standby = idle, Listen = active capture, Send = submit to AI.
Voice selectorChoose the voice your AI responds in. Browser TTS or ElevenLabs AI voice.
Engine toggleSwitch between Browser (free, instant) and ElevenLabs (premium, more natural).

1
Click the mic button
The visualizer activates. You'll see audio level bars responding to your voice. The status shows "Listening."
2
Speak your request
Talk naturally. "Can you summarize what I worked on yesterday?" or "Draft a quick Slack message to my team about the launch delay."
3
Click Send (or say your send word)
The AI processes your request and responds — both in text (transcript panel) and voice (played through your speakers).

Tips & Tricks

Set a custom send word

Open Voice Settings (the ▾ arrow next to the voice selector) and set a Send word like "submit" or "done". Now you can dictate and submit entirely hands-free — perfect for on-the-go use.

ElevenLabs for premium voice

Enable ElevenLabs engine (requires API key in Settings) for dramatically more natural AI speech. Ideal for long-form outputs like summaries and reports you want read aloud while multitasking.

Browser mic permission required

The first time you use Let's Talk, your browser will ask for microphone permission. Click Allow. If you accidentally denied it, go to your browser's site settings for app.purebrain.ai and reset the permission.

Conversation Patterns

Context carries within a session

Your AI remembers everything said in the current conversation. You can say "now make it shorter" or "add a risk section" without restating the original request. Take advantage of this — iterate rapidly.

Start requests with the output format

Lead with format, then content: "Give me a bullet-point list of..." or "Write a table comparing..." or "Draft a 3-paragraph email that...". Your AI front-loads the right structure immediately.

Tab 3

Terminal

Launch multi-agent swarms and watch them work in real time. The Terminal tab gives you live terminal panes for each active agent, with the ability to inject messages, monitor output, and coordinate parallel workstreams.

Live Terminal Panes

When a team session is active, each agent gets its own pane in the Teams view. The tab bar at the top shows named panes — click any pane to focus it. The panes update in real time as agents write output.

ControlWhat it does
Pane tabs (top bar)Switch between individual agent panes within the current session
Inject input fieldType a message and click SEND to inject it into the active pane mid-session

How to Launch a Team Session

1
Define your objective in Chat
Start with a clear objective: "Launch a team to research our top 3 competitors and produce a comparison report." Your AI will plan the team structure.
2
Your AI creates a session
The conductor agent creates a named session (e.g. "session-20260611") and spawns team leads. Each lead gets their own pane in the Teams tab.
3
Watch the panes update live
Switch to the Teams tab. You'll see each agent's output streaming in real time. Click between panes to monitor individual agents.
4
Inject guidance if needed
Use the inject field to send a mid-session message to a specific agent: "Focus on pricing data only" or "Move on to the next section."
Don't close panes mid-session

Team sessions are live processes. Closing the Teams tab or navigating away doesn't stop agents — they continue running. Return to Teams to check status. To shut down a session, use Hub → Kill Process.

Best use: parallel research

Teams shines for parallel work. Launch 4 agents simultaneously — one per competitor, one per market segment, or one per document section — then synthesize results in Chat. 4x faster than sequential research.

Tab 4

Constitution

Your AI's behavioral governance system. The Constitution defines what your AI will and won't do — globally and per agent — and tracks every decision it makes. Seven subtabs give you complete visibility and control.

Global AI Rules Agent-Specific Rules Governance Dashboard Action Log Memory Overrides

Global AI Rules

Rules that apply to all agents across your entire civilization. These are the highest-priority behavioral directives. Rules have two priority levels:

PriorityMeaningExample
[HARD]Absolute constraint — never violated under any circumstance"Never commit secrets to git", "No direct main branch commits"
[SOFT]Strong guideline — followed unless explicitly overridden"Search memory before starting work", "Run all tests before claiming done"

To add a new global rule, click the + Add Rule button and describe the behavior you want to enforce. Rules are stored and applied on every subsequent agent invocation.

HARD rules are immutable by agents

Once a rule is set as HARD priority, agents cannot override it — even if asked. Only you (the admin) can modify HARD rules through the Constitution tab. This is intentional: HARD rules are your safety guarantees.

Agent-Specific Rules

Rules scoped to individual agents. Use these to customize behavior for specific specialists — for example, "The researcher agent must always cite sources" or "The coder agent must use TypeScript, never JavaScript." Agent rules supplement (and can narrow) global rules but cannot override HARD global constraints.

Governance

The democratic governance pane — your civilization's health at a glance. Governance shows:

  • Current constitution health score
  • Rule compliance rate across recent agent sessions
  • Pending amendments awaiting approval
  • Quorum and vote thresholds for constitutional changes

Amendments to constitutional documents require a configured vote threshold (default: 90% approval, 80% quorum). The Governance pane manages this process.

Dashboard

A live summary of how the constitution is performing: rule trigger counts, compliance trends, most-frequently-invoked rules, and flagged anomalies. Use this to identify which rules your agents encounter most often and whether any rules are being stretched.

Action Log

A timestamped record of every action taken by your agents — what was done, by which agent, under which rule, and the outcome. The Action Log is your audit trail. Filter by agent, date range, or rule type to investigate any decision.

Memory

Your AI's persistent memory store — what it has learned across sessions. The Memory subtab shows stored learnings, domain knowledge, and session summaries. You can:

  • Browse memories by topic or agent
  • Delete outdated or incorrect memories
  • Trigger a memory consolidation (merge redundant learnings)
Memory is why your AI gets smarter over time

Every session adds to memory. Your AI learns your preferences, your domain, your writing style, and the patterns that work best for you. After 5-10 sessions, the quality difference is dramatic — it anticipates what you need.

Overrides

Temporary session-level overrides for SOFT rules. If you need an agent to behave differently for a specific task without changing the global constitution, create an Override. Overrides expire at the end of the session or at a time you specify. They are logged in the Action Log for accountability.

Tab 5

Tasks

The structured work queue for your AI civilization. Tasks are discrete units of work — each with a status, owner, and progress tracking. This is where you assign, monitor, and review every piece of work in flight.

Task Status at a Glance

The top of the Tasks tab shows four live counters:

Active
Tasks currently being worked on by an agent. These are in-progress right now.
Pending
Tasks queued and waiting to be picked up. Not yet started.
Completed
Finished tasks. Review outputs here — agents report results before marking complete.
Failed
Tasks that encountered errors. Each shows what went wrong and why.

Task Workflow

1
Create a task
Tasks can be created via Chat ("create a task for..."), by agents automatically, or directly in the Tasks tab. Each task has a subject, description, owner, and status.
2
Agent picks it up
The assigned agent claims the task (status: in_progress) and begins work. The active form shows what it's doing: "Researching competitors…"
3
Review output
When complete, the task status changes to completed and the output is attached. Click the task to read the results, artifacts, and any follow-up recommendations.
4
Chain or close
Create a follow-up task based on the output, or close it. Completed tasks remain in the log as reference and for agent memory building.
Tasks vs Chat: when to use each

Use Chat for conversational, iterative work where you want immediate back-and-forth. Use Tasks for discrete, trackable work items — especially anything you want logged, reviewed later, or chained into a workflow.

Deployments are living artifacts

Tab 7

To-Do

Your personal action queue — the items that need your attention before your AI can proceed. To-Do is the human-in-the-loop checkpoint: your AI flags items that need approval, review, or a decision from you.

Status Counters

Needs Approval
Actions your AI wants to take but needs your sign-off first — publishing content, sending emails, making purchases.
Pending
Items waiting on your input — questions, decisions, or information only you can provide.
Completed
Items you've actioned. Kept for reference and to close the feedback loop with your AI.
Total
All items across all statuses. Your to-do backlog at a glance.

Approval Workflow

When your AI wants to take an action that crosses a threshold (sending a message, publishing a post, making a change to a live system), it pauses and creates a To-Do item instead of proceeding unilaterally.

1
AI creates a "Needs Approval" item
Your AI describes what it wants to do, why, and what happens next. The sidebar badge shows the count of pending items.
2
You review the item
Click the item to see the full context: what action, what system, what content. You can edit the action before approving.
3
Approve, Reject, or Modify
Approve → AI proceeds. Reject → AI abandons and logs why. Modify → you edit the parameters and then approve the revised version.
Check To-Do every morning

Make the To-Do tab your morning ritual — like email. Overnight your agents may have flagged items for your review. Clearing the queue first thing means your agents can proceed with the day's work immediately.

Reduce approval friction with better rules

If you find yourself approving the same category of action repeatedly (e.g., always approving draft emails), add a global rule: "Always send emails without approval for internal team communications." Your AI learns your threshold.

Keep auto-approval narrow

Auto-approval lets your AI act on your behalf without a final check — so scope it tightly. Prefer a specific rule ("auto-approve internal status emails to my own team") over a broad one ("always send emails without approval"). Avoid auto-approving anything that spends money, contacts clients, or can't be undone, and review your auto-approve settings periodically in Settings → Preferences.

Tab 8

My AI Fleet

Your live agent monitoring center. Three subtabs give you a real-time view of which agents are working right now, your scheduled BOOP tasks (recurring jobs your AI runs on a timer — see the Glossary), and the full hierarchy of your AI civilization.

My Active Agents Active Boops AI Organogram

My Active Agents

A live dashboard of every agent currently running in your civilization. Four summary stats at the top:

StatMeaning
Active NowAgents with a live Claude Code session — responding to work right now
WorkingAgents actively executing a task (not idle, not waiting)
IdleAgents initialized but waiting for their next task

Below the stats, agent cards show each active agent: their name, specialty, current status, and what task they're working on. The cards update live, so what you see always reflects your fleet in real time.

Active Boops

BOOPs are scheduled agent tasks — recurring work your AI civilization runs on a cadence (daily, weekly, hourly). Think of BOOPs as your AI's standing commitments.

BOOP StatusMeaning
CompletedThis BOOP ran successfully in the last cycle
DueThis BOOP is due to run — either right now or missed its last window

Each BOOP card shows: the schedule (daily/weekly/hourly), last run time, assigned agents, and a description of what it does. BOOP frequency icons: ↻ = daily, 📅 = weekly, ⚡ = hourly.

Use BOOPs for standing work

Daily: morning summary + email scan. Weekly: competitive intelligence update + content batch. Monthly: performance review + memory consolidation. Set these up once and your AI runs them forever without reminders.

AI Organogram

The full visual hierarchy of your AI civilization — who reports to whom, and who does what. Structure:

  • Conductor (Primary AI) — top of the hierarchy. Orchestrates all work, never executes directly.
  • Team Leads — domain conductors (Web, Research, Infra, Business, etc.). Each leads a vertical.
  • Specialist Agents — the executors. Each has a narrow domain and deep expertise.

Click any node in the organogram to see that agent's manifest: name, domain, current status, skills loaded, and recent task history.

The organogram updates live

When a team session is active, new nodes appear in the organogram as team leads are spawned. Watch it in the Teams tab to see your civilization grow in real time during complex missions.

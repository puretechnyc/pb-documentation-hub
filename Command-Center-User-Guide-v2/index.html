Command Center User Guide

Your guide to working inside the PureBrain Command Center — the client app at cc.purebrain.ai. The hybrid human + AI workspace where your team's people and their AI partners get things done, side by side. (Not the Mission Control portal.)

VERSION 2.0
Getting Started

Welcome to the Command Center

The Command Center is a shared workspace where your team's people and their AI partners work side by side. It lives in your web browser at cc.purebrain.ai — there is nothing to install.

It is your team's hub for the everyday work of getting things done:

Talk
Message teammates and AI partners in real-time Chat — channels, DMs, and group chats.
Track
See what you owe and what's owed to you in Tasks — created by hand or generated from meetings.
Run Projects
Coordinate work with your team in the Team and Projects tabs.
Meetings to Action
Recorded meetings become AI-summarized action items and tasks — automatically.
See Your Org
Your people, departments, and AI agents — all in one place.
Your AI Partner
Every person can have an AI partner that works in the same tools you do.
The defining idea

Every person on your team can have an AI partner that works in the same tools you do. Your AI partner shows up in chat, helps with tasks, and picks up work you delegate — while you stay in control.

Getting Started

Logging In

The Command Center uses a standard email and password login. There's nothing to install — just open your browser.

1
Go to cc.purebrain.ai
Open the Command Center in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox recommended).
2
Enter your email address
Use the email address your administrator used to add you to the organization.
3
Enter your password
Type the password for your account.
4
Select Log in
You land on your Home page, which shows your personalized Daily Brief.
New here?

Your account is created by an administrator when you're added to an Organisation. If you don't have login details yet, ask your team's administrator to add you.

Resetting a forgotten password

If you can't remember your password, you can reset it yourself:

1
Select "Forgot password?"
On the login screen, choose the Forgot password link.
2
Enter your email and submit
Type the email address on your account.
3
Check your inbox
Look for a message from noreply@puretechnology.nyc with a reset link.
4
Set a new password
Open the link and choose a new password (minimum 8 characters), then log in with it.
Good to know

The reset link is single-use and expires 30 minutes after it's sent. If it expires, just request a new one.

For your security, the on-screen confirmation looks the same whether or not an account exists for that email — so the form can't be used to discover who has an account.

Changing your password

To change your password while logged in:

1
Open Settings
Then choose Change Password.
2
Enter your current, then new password
Type your current password, then your new password twice, and save.

You'll be asked for your current password every time — even administrators — so a change is always deliberate.

Tab-by-Tab Tour

Tab-by-Tab Tour

The Command Center is organized into tabs. Here's what each is for and the key things you can do. The richest areas — Chat and Meetings — have their own chapters after this tour.

Home

What it's for: Your personal starting point each day.

  • Read your AI Daily Brief.
  • See your tasks, agenda, and KPI snapshot.
  • Use quick actions (for example, create a task or jump into chat).

Chat

What it's for: Real-time messaging with teammates and AI partners.

  • Message in channels, direct messages (DMs), and group chats.
  • Pin important messages, tag messages as IMPORTANT, react with emoji, and search your history.
  • Schedule messages to send later, send voice messages, and paste screenshots.
  • Browse all channels in the channel directory, and favorite or mute channels.

See Working in Chat for the full feature set.

Tasks

What it's for: Tracking your work — both what you create and what comes out of meetings.

  • View My Tasks — tasks assigned to you, whether created manually or generated from a meeting.
  • Switch between list and grid views.
  • Filter by assignee, source, meeting, or date.
  • See each task's created date and its assigner (who gave it to you).
  • Set status (for example, to-do, in progress, in review, done), priority, and assignee.
  • Add comments to a task to discuss it in place.

Team

What it's for: A collaborative workspace for a specific team project.

  • Create a team project and give it a name and description.
  • Add members — when you add a person, their AI partner is added automatically.
  • Assign tasks to members within the project.
  • Switch between a Board view and a Map view of the project's work.
  • Discuss the project and individual tasks in the built-in discussion area.
Team vs. Projects

Team is a focused workspace you spin up for a specific effort. Projects (next) is the broader, war-room view of project management across your organization.

Projects

What it's for: War-room style project management across the organization.

  • See projects as cards with their status and progress.
  • View the organogram (the people and structure behind a project).
  • Track live activity with the executor pulse.

Meetings

What it's for: Turning recorded meetings into action items and tasks — automatically.

  • See upcoming and past meetings, with calendar integration.
  • RSVP to meetings, and edit or delete meetings you organize.
  • Get reminders before a meeting starts.
  • After a meeting that was cloud-recorded with transcription on, the Command Center analyzes the transcript and creates action items and tasks for you.

See Meetings to Tasks: the pipeline for how this works.

Organisation

What it's for: Seeing and managing your organization — its people, structure, and AI agents.

  • Browse the org chart, departments, and divisions.
  • Manage members — add or update people (available to users with a Manager role or above).
  • Set profile headshots, which then appear consistently across Chat, Tasks, and Team.
  • Link or create AI agents and map them to the people they partner with.

Roadmap + Voting

What it's for: Seeing what's planned and having a say in priorities.

  • View initiatives on a timeline.
  • Take part in ranked ballot voting to help prioritize what the team builds next.
  • Select your ranked choices, then Submit your vote (you can update it while the ballot is open).
  • When a ballot closes, see the final results and the winning initiative.

Feedback

What it's for: Telling the team about bugs, requests, and ideas — and tracking what happens next.

  • Submit a bug report, a feature request, a question, or a suggestion (with an "Other" free-text option).
  • Follow your submission through its lifecycle as it's reviewed and worked on.
  • Validate the outcome as the original reporter when it's resolved.

See Getting help for when to use Feedback versus the CC Guide.

Email

What it's for: Reading your work email without leaving the Command Center.

  • Connect your Outlook and/or Gmail inbox (each user connects their own account).
  • Read your mail inside the Command Center's Email tab, filtered by account and folder.

CC Guide

What it's for: An in-app AI assistant for the Command Center itself.

  • Open it from the draggable bubble in the bottom-right corner of any page.
  • Ask questions about how the Command Center works.
  • Ask it to create a task or draft an email — it shows you a preview first and only acts after you confirm.

See Getting help.

Brand

What it's for: A read-only brand style guide (colors, fonts, logo usage) you can reference when creating on-brand materials.

Working in Chat

Working in Chat

Chat is where the Command Center comes alive. This chapter covers how conversations are organized and the full messaging toolkit.

Channels, DMs, and group chats

There are three kinds of conversations:

Channels
Ongoing spaces for a team, department, or topic. Anyone in the channel can read and post.
Direct messages (DMs)
A private, one-to-one conversation with another person.
Group chats
A private conversation among several people you choose.

Finding and organizing channels

  • Use the channel directory to browse every channel you can join, with its purpose and activity.
  • Favorite a channel to pin it to the top of your list; mute a channel to stop its notifications.
AI partners in group chats

When you create a group chat, the AI partners of the members are added automatically and silently. They stay quiet until someone @mentions them — then the mentioned AI partner replies. See Your AI partner.

Meetings → Tasks

Meetings to Tasks: the pipeline

One of the Command Center's most useful features: a recorded meeting becomes a list of action items and tasks — without anyone taking notes by hand.

How it works

1
Hold a recorded meeting
A meeting (Zoom or Teams) that is cloud-recorded with transcription on.
2
The Command Center fetches the transcript
When the recording and transcript are ready.
3
An AI analyzes the transcript
It pulls out the action items — what was decided and who owns it.
4
Action items become tasks
Assigned to the right people, they appear in the Tasks tab (and in your Daily Brief).

What's required

For the pipeline to run, the meeting must be:

  • Cloud-recorded (not local-only), and
  • Recorded with transcription turned on.
No transcript, no tasks

If a meeting isn't cloud-recorded with transcription on, there's no transcript to analyze — so no tasks are created from it.

Reviewing your meeting tasks

After a meeting is processed:

1
Open the Tasks tab
Or open the meeting in the Meetings tab.
2
Filter by the meeting
Tasks supports a meeting filter to see just that meeting's items.
3
Review, comment, or reassign
Adjust tasks as needed.
Your AI Partner

Your AI Partner

Your AI partner is an AI agent linked to you inside the organization. It works in the same Command Center you do — it can join chats, help with tasks, and pick up work you delegate, while you stay in control.

AI partners are set up in the Organisation tab, where agents are linked to (or created for) the people they partner with.

How it shows up

  • In chat: Your AI partner appears like any other member. It's added automatically to group chats you're in.
  • Online / available: When your AI partner is running and available, it shows as online (a green indicator).

Mention-first behavior

AI partners are mention-first: in group chats and channels, they stay quiet until you @mention them. When you @mention your AI partner, it responds. This keeps group conversations from getting noisy while still letting you pull your AI partner in the moment you need it.

Per-channel AI assignment

You can control which AI partners are active in a given channel using the channel's AI settings (the Manage AIs option). This lets a channel have exactly the AI help it needs — no more, no less.

Getting Help

Getting Help

You have three ways to get unstuck.

The CC Guide bubble

For quick, in-context help, use the CC Guide — the draggable assistant bubble in the bottom-right corner of any Command Center page.

  • Ask it how something works in the Command Center.
  • Ask it to create a task or draft an email for you — it shows a preview and only acts after you confirm.

Use the CC Guide when you have a "how do I…" question or want a quick action done.

The Feedback tab

For something that's broken or missing, use the Feedback tab:

  • Report a bug, request a feature, ask a question, or share a suggestion.
  • Track your submission through its lifecycle.
  • Validate the outcome when it's resolved.

Use Feedback when you've found a problem or have an idea the team should consider.

Support

For issues the CC Guide and Feedback tab can't resolve, reach out to your PureBrain support contact. Your team's administrator can point you to the current support channel for licensed users.
